## Improvements

- Improved filtering on the My Auctions page.
- Optimized the loading speed of the My Auctions page.
- Fixed various bugs, including issues related to specific edge cases.
- Added a **System** label for bids placed automatically by proxy bidding in the bid history modal, making it easier for customers to understand how they were outbid.
- Added a **Load more** option to the My Auctions page as a fallback when pagination is not triggered.
