The **Bidder Profile** block appears on a customer’s details page in Shopify Admin. It gives your team a quick way to manage whether that customer can participate in auctions and to open their bidding activity without leaving the customer record.

:::info

Use the Bidder Profile block when you want to review or update a customer’s auction access directly from **Shopify Admin → Customers**.

:::

## What you can do with the Bidder Profile block

- See whether a customer is currently allowed to bid
- Change the customer’s bidder status
- Open the customer’s bidding activity from the same block

## Add the Bidder Profile block to a customer page

Before you can manage auction access for a customer, you need to add the Reflow Auctions block to the customer details page in Shopify Admin.

1. **Open Customers in Shopify Admin**

   Go to **Shopify Admin → Customers**, then open any customer record.

2. **Find the Blocks section**

   Scroll down on the customer details page until you see the **Blocks** section.

3. **Add the Reflow Auctions block**

   Click **\+ Block**, then select **Reflow Auctions**.

4. **Confirm the Bidder Profile block appears**

   After adding the block, the **Bidder Profile** block is placed on the customer page automatically.

5. **Pin it for your team if needed**

   If you want the block to appear for everyone on your staff, click **Pin to pages for all staff**.

:::tip

If your team regularly reviews auction bidders, pinning the block for all staff helps keep the customer page consistent across your admin.

:::

## Use the Bidder Profile block

Once the block has been added, you can manage the customer’s auction participation directly from their customer record.

### View bidder status

The block shows the customer’s current **Bidder status**, so you can quickly confirm whether they are allowed to participate in auctions.

### Update bidder status

Use the status dropdown in the block to change the customer’s auction access.

- **Active** — the customer can participate in auctions
- **Inactive/Banned** — the customer cannot participate in auctions

### Open the customer’s bids

Click **Customer’s bids** to review the customer’s bidding activity.

:::note

Changing a customer’s bidder status affects their ability to take part in auctions going forward. Use this setting when you need to allow or restrict bidding access for a specific customer.

:::

## At a glance

- Add the block from the **Blocks** section on any customer details page
- Use the block to view and update the customer’s bidder status
- Open **Customer’s bids** to review bidding activity
