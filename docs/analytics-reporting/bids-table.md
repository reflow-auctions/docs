The **Bids Table** gives admins a detailed, searchable view of bidding activity across all auctions. Use it to review who placed a bid, how much was submitted, what kind of bid it was, and when it happened.

:::info

The Bids Table includes both customer bidding activity and system-recorded bid events, so you can track auction participation and completed purchase actions in one place.

:::

![Bids Table showing search, filters, and bid activity across auctions](/img/docs/analytics-reporting/bids-table-1.png)

## What you can do in the Bids Table

- **Search by bidder** to quickly find activity from a specific customer.
- **Filter results** to narrow the list and focus on the bids you need to review.
- **Compare activity across auctions** from one centralized view.
- **Review purchase methods** such as standard bids, Buy It Now purchases, and Make Offer purchases.
- **Check bid timing** to confirm when bidding activity took place.

## Understand the table columns

### Bid ID

A unique identifier for each bid record. This is useful when referencing a specific bid internally.

### Customer

The name of the bidder who placed the bid or completed the action.

### Amount

The value submitted for the bid or purchase event.

### Type

This column shows what kind of activity was recorded:

- **Bid** — A standard bid submitted by a customer.
- **Buy it now** — A completed purchase made using the Buy It Now option.
- **Make offer** — A completed purchase made through the Make Offer option.
- **System** — A system-generated bid event, such as a proxy bidding action.

### Auction

The auction where the bid or purchase activity occurred.

### Date and Time

The exact date and timestamp when the bid or event was recorded.

## How to use the Bids Table

1. **Open the Bids page**

   Go to the app admin and open **Bids** from the auction navigation.

2. **Search for a bidder**

   Use the **Search by bidder** field to find bid activity for a specific customer.

3. **Apply filters**

   Select **Add filter** to narrow the results and focus on the records most relevant to your review.

4. **Review bid details**

   Scan the table to compare bid amounts, bid types, auction names, and timestamps.

## When to use this page

The Bids Table is especially helpful when you need to:

- Investigate bidding activity for a specific customer
- Review participation across multiple auctions
- Confirm whether an action was a bid, Buy It Now purchase, Make Offer purchase, or system event
- Verify the timing of bidding and purchase activity

:::tip

If you are reviewing a large volume of activity, start with the bidder search and then apply filters to reduce the list before comparing results.

:::

:::note

The Bids Table is intended for visibility and tracking. Depending on your workflow, some bid outcomes or customer details may be managed elsewhere in the app.

:::
