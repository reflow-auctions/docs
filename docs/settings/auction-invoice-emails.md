When a customer wins an auction, Reflow Auctions emails them an invoice with a button to complete their purchase. Use **Auction invoice emails** to customize that email for each language your customers use.

:::info

Changes apply to all future auction invoice emails sent in that language, including invoices you [resend from the Invoices page](/bidding-purchasing/invoices#resend-an-invoice).

:::

![Settings Notifications page showing the Auction invoice emails card with English and Spanish tabs, the Subject, Custom message and Checkout button text fields, and the Set Spanish as default checkbox](/img/docs/settings/auction-invoice-emails-1.png)

## What you can customize

Each language has its own version of the email with three fields:

| Field | What it changes |
|---|---|
| **Subject** | The email subject line. Required. |
| **Custom message** | The text at the top of the email, above the checkout button. |
| **Checkout button text** | The label on the button that takes the customer to checkout. Required. |

The field labels are shown in the language you're editing. For example, on the **Spanish** tab, **Subject** appears as **Asunto**.

If you leave **Custom message** empty, the email uses the built-in default text for that language.

**English** and **Spanish** are available.

## Customize the invoice email

#### 1. Open the Notifications settings

In the Reflow Auctions app, go to **Settings → Notifications**.

#### 2. Choose a language

In the **Auction invoice emails** card, select the **English** or **Spanish** tab.

#### 3. Edit the email

Update the **Subject**, **Custom message** and **Checkout button text** for that language.

#### 4. Preview the email

Click **Preview** to see how the email will look to customers in the selected language.

#### 5. Save your changes

Click **Save** to apply your changes.

Repeat these steps for each language you want to customize.

## Choose the default language

By default, each customer receives the invoice email in their own language. If their language isn't available, the email is sent in your store's primary language, or in English if that isn't available either.

To send every invoice email in one language instead, open that language's tab, select **Set [language] as default**, for example **Set Spanish as default**, and save. The default language is marked with a **Default** badge.

:::warning

When a default language is set, **all** customers receive the invoice email in that language, whatever language they use in your store. Clear the checkbox if you want each customer to get the email in their own language.

:::
