Every important change in an event or auction is recorded in the **Activity** section so you can review what happened, when it happened, and who triggered it. This gives your team a clear history of updates, bidder activity, and system actions.

:::info

Activity logs are read-only records designed to help you verify auction progress and review past actions with full transparency.

:::

## Where to find activity logs

#### 1. Open the Reflow Auctions app

From your Shopify admin, open **Reflow Auctions**.

#### 2. Select the event or auction

In the left navigation, open **Events** or **Auctions**, then choose the item you want to review.

#### 3. Scroll to the Activity section

On the event or auction details page, scroll down to the **Activity** timeline to view the full log history.

![Auction details page showing the Activity section timeline beneath bid information and auction settings](/img/docs/managing-auctions/activity-logs-1.png)

## What you can see in the log

The activity log shows a chronological timeline of actions related to the selected event or auction. Depending on what has happened, the log may include:

- **Auction lifecycle updates**, such as creation, start, completion, or status changes
- **Buy it now activity**, including when purchasing was happened
- **Order-related actions**, such as a draft order being created for a winning bidder
- **Administrative changes**, including updates to auction settings or event details

## How to read the timeline

Each log entry is displayed in time order so you can quickly understand the sequence of events.

- **Date grouping** helps separate activity by day
- **Timestamps** show exactly when each action occurred
- **Entry descriptions** explain the action in plain language

:::tip

Use the activity log when you need to confirm why an auction changed status, or whether an order was created after the auction ended.

:::

## When activity logs are useful

- Checking whether an auction finished as expected
- Reviewing bidder and order activity after an auction closes
- Confirming the timing of changes made by your team
- Investigating questions about auction outcomes or event history

## Things to keep in mind

:::note

The activity log is meant to help you review historical actions. It is not an editable area, and entries cannot be changed from the activity timeline.

:::

:::warning

If you are troubleshooting an auction, review the full timeline instead of relying on a single entry. Related actions, such as bids, status updates, and order creation, may appear as separate log items.

:::

## Summary

The **Event and Auction Activity Logs** give you a clear, time-based record of what happened in each event or auction. Use the timeline to monitor changes and review important actions with confidence.
