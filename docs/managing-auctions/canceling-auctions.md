You can cancel any **Scheduled** or **Running** auction at any time, even if bids have already been placed.

:::info

Canceling an auction takes effect immediately. Shoppers will see the auction as **Canceled**, bidding stops right away, and the item can no longer be purchased through that auction.

:::

### What happens when an auction is canceled

- The auction status changes to **Canceled** in the admin.
- The auction page on the storefront updates immediately to show that it has been canceled.
- Customers can no longer place bids.
- The item is no longer available for purchase through the auction, even if a customer had already added it to their cart before the cancellation.

![Canceled auction in the admin showing the Keep auction button on the Auction Details page](/img/docs/managing-auctions/canceling-auctions-1.png)

### Cancel an auction

#### 1. Open the auction

In the admin, go to the auction you want to stop and open its **Auction Details** page.

#### 2. Cancel the auction

Cancel the auction from the details view. The auction is marked as **Canceled** immediately.

#### 3. Confirm the storefront result

On the storefront, customers will see the auction as canceled and will no longer be able to bid or complete a purchase for that auction item.

![Storefront view of a canceled auction showing the auction status as Canceled](/img/docs/managing-auctions/canceling-auctions-2.png)

### Re-publish a canceled auction

If you want to make a canceled auction active again, you can re-publish it from the same auction record.

#### 1. Open Auction Details

In the admin, open the canceled auction's **Auction Details** page.

#### 2. Click Keep Auction

Select **Keep auction** to re-publish the auction.

#### 3. Auction goes live again

The auction becomes available on the storefront right away.

:::note

On the shopper side, the auction is re-published immediately after you click **Keep auction**.

:::

:::tip

If shoppers were previously watching the auction, they can resume bidding as soon as the auction becomes active again.

:::

### Important behavior to know

- Cancellation is available for both **Scheduled** and **Running** auctions.
- Placed bids do not prevent cancellation.
- Re-publishing restores access to the auction for shoppers as soon as it is active again.
