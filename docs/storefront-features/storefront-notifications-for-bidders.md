## Notifications for bidders

Notifications help bidders stay on top of important updates across auctions, invoices, and orders without having to check each item manually. From the **My Auctions** page, bidders can open the **Notifications** panel to review recent activity, see what needs attention, and jump directly to the related item.

Bidders receive notifications for important events such as auction updates, invoice activity, and order-related changes.

:::info

Notifications are designed for bidders and show activity that may require review or action.

:::

### Where bidders can find notifications

On the **My Auctions** page, a **Notifications** icon appears in the top-right area of the page. Selecting it opens the **Notification Center** in a modal window.

![image](/img/docs/storefront-features/storefront-notifications-for-bidders-1.png)

The panel lets bidders review updates without leaving the page. If a notification is linked to an auction, invoice, or order, bidders can open it directly from the notification.

### How notifications work

#### 1. Open the Notification Center

Go to **My Auctions** and select the **Notifications** icon.

#### 2. Choose a notification tab

Use the tabs at the top of the panel to view **All**, **Auctions**, **Invoices**, or **Orders** notifications.

#### 3. Review unread activity

Unread notifications are highlighted with a visual indicator so bidders can quickly spot new activity.

#### 4. Open the related item

Select a notification to open the related auction, invoice, or order. After it is opened, the notification is marked as read.

:::tip

Checking notifications regularly helps bidders respond faster to bid changes, invoice reminders, and order updates.

:::

### What bidders will see in each notification

Each notification includes the details bidders need to understand what happened and decide what to do next.

![image](/img/docs/storefront-features/storefront-notifications-for-bidders-2.png)

- A message that explains the update
- A timestamp showing when the event happened
- Related auction, invoice, or order details when available
- Direct navigation to the affected item

### Notification categories

#### All

Shows every recent notification in one place.

#### Auctions

Shows auction-related updates, such as pricing changes, auction endings, and bid outcomes.

#### Invoices

Shows invoice activity, including new invoices and payment reminders.

#### Orders

Shows order-related updates when an order requires attention or changes status.

If there are no notifications in the selected category, the panel displays an empty state so bidders know there is nothing new to review.

### Supported bidder notifications

Bidders may see notifications for activity such as:

- Changes to an auction they are following or participating in
- Updates to bids or auction outcomes
- New invoices or invoice reminders
- Order status changes or order-related actions that need attention

:::note

The exact notifications available may depend on the activity in the account and the types of auctions, invoices, and orders associated with the bidder.

:::
