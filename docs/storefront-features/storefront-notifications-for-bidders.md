## Notifications for bidders

Notifications help bidders stay on top of important updates across auctions, invoices, and orders without having to check each item manually. Bidders can review their notifications in two places:

- **Notification Center**: a panel that opens from the **Notifications** bell icon on Reflow Auctions storefront pages.
- **Auction Notifications page**: a full storefront page with all of a bidder's notifications. You can add it to your store menu.

Both show the same notifications, so bidders can use whichever is more convenient.

:::info

Notifications are designed for bidders and show activity that may require review or action. Bidders need to be signed in to their customer account to see them.

:::

### Where bidders can find notifications

A **Notifications** bell icon appears in the top-right area of Reflow Auctions storefront pages, such as **My Auctions** and **Auctions**. A dot on the icon shows that there are unread notifications. Selecting the icon opens the **Notification Center** in a modal window.

![My Auctions page on a phone with an arrow pointing to the Notifications bell icon in the top-right corner](/img/docs/storefront-features/storefront-notifications-for-bidders-1.png)

![Auctions page with the Notifications bell icon and an unread dot next to the My Auctions and Items summary links](/img/docs/storefront-features/storefront-notifications-for-bidders-4.png)

The panel lets bidders review updates without leaving the page. If a notification is linked to an auction, invoice, or order, bidders can open it directly from the notification.

If you've added the **Auction Notifications** page to your store menu, bidders can also open it from the menu. See [Auction Notifications page](#auction-notifications-page).

### How notifications work

#### 1. Open the Notification Center

Select the **Notifications** bell icon on a Reflow Auctions storefront page, or open the **Auction Notifications** page from your store menu.

#### 2. Choose a notification tab

Use the tabs at the top to view **All**, **Auctions**, **Invoices**, or **Orders** notifications. Each tab shows how many unread notifications it has.

#### 3. Review unread activity

Unread notifications are highlighted with a visual indicator so bidders can quickly spot new activity. To clear them all at once, select **Mark all as read**.

#### 4. Open the related item

Select a notification to open the related auction, invoice, or order. After it is opened, the notification is marked as read.

:::tip

Checking notifications regularly helps bidders respond faster to bid changes, invoice reminders, and order updates.

:::

### What bidders will see in each notification

Each notification includes the details bidders need to understand what happened and decide what to do next.

![Notification Center panel with All, Auctions, Invoices and Orders tabs and a list of invoice, auction ended, won item and outbid notifications](/img/docs/storefront-features/storefront-notifications-for-bidders-2.png)

- A message that explains the update
- How long ago it happened, for example **5d ago**
- Related auction, invoice, or order details when available
- Direct navigation to the affected item

### Notification categories

#### All

Shows every recent notification in one place.

#### Auctions

Shows auction-related updates, such as Buy It Now price changes, auction endings, and bids that were outbid.

#### Invoices

Shows invoice activity, including new invoices, payment reminders, and invoices that were paid, expired, or cancelled.

#### Orders

Shows order-related updates, such as a completed payment for a won item.

If there are no notifications in the selected category, an empty state appears so bidders know there is nothing new to review.

### Supported bidder notifications

Bidders may see notifications when:

- Another bidder places a higher bid (**You've been outbid**)
- The Buy It Now price of an auction changes
- An auction or a group of auctions ends and winning bids are being determined
- An invoice is ready to pay
- An invoice is still unpaid (**Payment reminder**)
- An invoice is paid, expires, or is cancelled
- A payment is complete and the item is theirs

:::note

If an invoice has expired or was cancelled, its notification says that payment is no longer available.

:::

## Auction Notifications page

The **Auction Notifications** page shows each bidder their auction, invoice, and order notifications on one storefront page. It has the same tabs as the Notification Center, with more room to read and manage notifications.

The page is available on your store at `/apps/auctions/auction-notifications`. You'll also find the link on the **Auction Notifications** card in **Settings** > **Pages**. Bidders who aren't signed in see a sign-in screen.

### What bidders can do on the page

- Switch between the **All**, **Auctions**, **Invoices**, and **Orders** tabs, each with its unread count
- Select **Mark all as read** to clear every unread notification
- Open a notification's actions menu and select **Mark as read** or **Mark as unread**
- Select a notification to open the related item, for example **View auction**, **View invoice**, or **View order**
- Scroll down to load older notifications

### Add the Auction Notifications page to your store menu

Add the page to a store menu so bidders can find it easily.

![Settings Pages section with the Auction Notifications card showing the page link, an Added to menus label and the Main menu selected](/img/docs/storefront-features/storefront-notifications-for-bidders-3.png)

#### 1. Open the Pages settings

In Reflow Auctions, go to **Settings** and select **Pages**.

#### 2. Find the Auction Notifications card

Scroll to the **Auction Notifications** card. It shows the page link.

#### 3. Choose a menu

In **Menus**, select the store menu where the page should appear, for example **Main menu**.

#### 4. Add the page

Select **Install**. Once the page is in a menu, the card shows **Added to menus**. To move it to a different menu later, choose the menu and select **Update**.

To rename or reorder the menu item, select **Instructions to customize** on the card and follow the steps for your Shopify navigation.

:::tip

For signed-in bidders, the menu link to the **Auction Notifications** page shows a badge with their number of unread notifications.

:::
