Hello World

Reflow Auctions helps you manage auctions, review activity, and keep work moving in one place. Use this page for a quick overview of what the app does and how to find your way around.

## What you can do

- View active, upcoming, and completed auctions
- Open item, lot, or listing details
- Track bidding activity and auction progress
- Review statuses, timelines, and recent updates
- Work across the parts of the app that match your role

## How the app is organized

### Navigation

Use the main navigation to move between key areas, such as auctions, records, and operational views.

### Work area

The center of the screen shows the page you are working in, including lists, records, auction details, and available actions.

### Search and filters

Use search, filters, and sorting to quickly find the right auctions, items, or updates.

### Record details

Open any auction or related record to review its status, activity, timeline, and other key information.

:::note

If a feature described in the documentation is not visible in your account, it may depend on your permissions or account setup.

:::
