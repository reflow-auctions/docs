Reflow Auctions helps you run timed auctions directly in your Shopify store. You can group products into auction events, set bidding rules, and manage everything from one place while keeping your catalog and inventory connected to Shopify.

Use the app to sell products through live bidding, instant purchase options. Whether you run a single event or manage multiple auctions at once, Reflow Auctions gives you the tools to control pricing, monitor activity, and turn bids into orders.

:::info

Reflow Auctions is built for Shopify merchants who want to sell products through competitive bidding while still offering flexible checkout options like instant purchase and automated draft order handling.

:::

## What you can do with Reflow Auctions

- **Create auction events** for one or many products.
- **Set bidding rules** such as starting bid, bid increments, and auction length.
- **Offer multiple purchase paths** with features like Buy It Now and Make Offer.
- **Support advanced bidding** with Popcorn Bidding and Proxy/Auto Bidding.
- **Collect customer details** through customer notes and photo uploads when needed.
- **Monitor auctions in real time** so you can track bids, activity, and outcomes as they happen.
- **Automate draft order creation** for winning bids and instant purchases.
- **Stay aligned with Shopify** by working from your existing product catalog and inventory.

## Who this app is for

Reflow Auctions is designed for Shopify merchants who want to sell products through scheduled or ongoing auctions instead of using only fixed pricing.

It is especially useful if you sell:

- Limited-quantity products
- Collectibles or rare items
- Products where buyer competition can increase sale value
- Items that benefit from flexible selling options such as bidding, offers, or instant purchase

:::note

If you already manage products in Shopify, Reflow Auctions lets you turn those products into auction listings without rebuilding your store setup from scratch.

:::

## How Reflow Auctions works

1. **Create an auction event**

   Add the products you want to sell and organize them into an event. This helps you manage timing, rules, and product visibility in one place.

2. **Set your auction rules**

   Choose the starting bid, minimum bid increase, and how long the auction should run. You can also enable options like Buy It Now, or automatic bidding depending on how you want customers to buy.

3. **Launch and monitor bidding**

   Once your event is live, customers can place bids, use instant purchase options where available, and interact with the auction according to the settings you enabled. You can watch bid activity and auction status in real time.

4. **Complete sales and process orders**

   When an auction ends or a customer purchases instantly, Reflow Auctions can generate the related draft or completed order so you can move quickly into fulfillment.

## Key features

### Flexible purchase options

Not every customer wants to wait for an auction to end. Reflow Auctions supports multiple purchase methods so you can match different buying behaviors:

- **Buy It Now** for immediate purchase at a fixed price
- **Standard bidding** for timed auction participation

### Advanced bidding tools

Reflow Auctions includes bidding features that help create a smoother and more competitive experience:

- **Proxy/Auto Bidding** lets customers set a maximum bid so the system can bid for them up to their limit.
- **Popcorn Bidding** helps keep bidding fair by extending the auction when last-minute bids come in.

:::tip

Use Popcorn Bidding when you want to reduce “last-second” bid sniping and give all interested buyers a fair chance to respond.

:::

### Real-time auction management

You can track auction activity as it happens, including current bids, auction status, and results across individual auctions or multiple events. This helps you respond quickly and stay informed without switching between systems.

### Shopify integration

Reflow Auctions works with your Shopify product catalog and inventory, so you can manage auction sales without maintaining a separate product system.

## Benefits for your store

- **Give customers more ways to buy** through bidding, offers, and instant purchase options.
- **Manage events more efficiently** from a centralized view.
- **Reduce manual work** with automated draft order creation.
- **Stay accurate** by using your existing Shopify products and inventory.

## Best fit for common selling scenarios

### Selling one-of-a-kind or rare items

Auctions work well when each item has limited availability and customer demand can influence the final sale price.

### Running scheduled sales events

If you launch collections at specific times, you can group products into a single event and manage the timing and bidding rules together.

### Offering both bidding and instant purchase

You can combine auction bidding with Buy It Now or Make Offer so customers can choose the buying path that fits them best.

### Managing higher volumes of auction activity

Real-time tracking and automated order creation help you stay organized when multiple products or events are active at once.

## In summary

Reflow Auctions gives Shopify merchants a practical way to run online auctions without leaving their existing store workflow. You can launch auction events, configure bidding behavior, support instant purchase options, track activity live, and automatically turn completed sales into orders.

If your business depends on urgency, competition, or flexible sales formats, Reflow Auctions helps you manage the full auction process in one place.
