Before a product can be added to an auction, it must exist in your Shopify catalog. You can create products one at a time in Shopify, or generate multiple products at once from the bulk creation page in the app.

:::info

Products created through either method become available for admins to use when setting up auctions.

:::

### Create a Single Product in Shopify

Use this option when you want to add one product manually and control all product details individually.

#### 1. Open Products in Shopify Admin

In your Shopify admin, go to **Products**, then click **Add product**.

#### 2. Enter the product details

Complete the product information you want to use in your catalog:

- **Title** – Enter the product name.
- **Description** – Add formatted text, images, and product details.
- **Media** – Upload product images or videos.
- **Price** – Set the product price and, if needed, a compare-at price.
- **Inventory** – Enable inventory tracking and add SKU, barcode, quantity, and location if needed.
- **Shipping** – Add weight and shipping settings for physical products.
- **Variants** – Add options such as size, color, or style if the product comes in multiple versions.
- **Search engine listing** – Add an SEO title and description if you want to customize how the product appears in search results.

#### 3. Choose visibility settings

Set the product **Status**:

- **Active** – Publishes the product immediately.
- **Draft** – Saves the product without publishing it.

Then select the **Sales channels** where the product should be available.

#### 4. Save the product

Click **Save** to add the product to your Shopify catalog.

:::tip

If you plan to use the product in an auction right away, make sure the product is saved correctly and available in the sales channels your store uses.

:::

### Create Products in Bulk

The bulk creation page lets admins generate many products at once using a shared set of settings. This is useful when you need to prepare a large group of products quickly, while keeping naming and product setup consistent.

![image](/img/docs/products/creating-products-1.png)

Bulk creation helps reduce repetitive manual work, improves speed, and lowers the chance of entry mistakes when adding many products to the catalog.

#### 1. Enter a title prefix

In **Prefix**, enter the starting text for the product titles. The prefix can include letters, numbers, and symbols.

For example, if you enter `SummerPallet -` , the generated products may be named `SummerPallet01`, `SummerPallet02`, and so on.

#### 2. Set the quantity

In **Quantity**, enter how many products you want to generate.

#### 3. Choose the product status

Select how the new products should be saved:

- **Draft** – Products are created but not published.
- **Active** – Products are published immediately.

#### 4. Select sales channels

Choose the **Sales channels** where the generated products should be available.

#### 5. Assign collections if needed

Use **Collections** to add the generated products to one or more existing collections. You can search for and select the collections you want to use.

#### 6. Add tags if needed

In **Tags**, add one or more tags to help organize, filter, or identify the generated products later.

#### 7. Choose a theme template

Select the **Theme Template** to apply to all generated products. If you do not need a specific template, leave the default option as **Default product**.

#### 8. Start generation

Click **Start** to begin creating the products in bulk.

:::note

Bulk creation is best for products that share the same basic setup. If each product needs unique descriptions, media, pricing, or variants, creating them individually in Shopify may be the better option.

:::

### Field Reference for Bulk Creation

- **Prefix** – The shared beginning of each generated product title.
- **Quantity** – The total number of products to create.
- **Status** – Whether products are created as draft or active.
- **Sales Channels** – Where the products will be available.
- **Collections** – Optional collection assignment during creation.
- **Tags** – Optional labels for organization and filtering.
- **Theme Template** – The product template applied to all generated items.

### Best Practices

- Use a clear naming prefix so generated products are easy to find later.
- Create products as **Draft** first if you want to review them before publishing.
- Apply tags during bulk creation to make filtering and management easier.
- Double-check sales channel selection so products appear only where intended.

:::warning

If you bulk-create products as **Active**, they may become available immediately on the selected sales channels. Use **Draft** if you want to review them first.

:::

### After Products Are Created

Once products exist in Shopify, admins can select them in the app and add them to an auction. If a product does not appear where expected, confirm that it was saved successfully, has the correct status, and is available on the appropriate sales channels.
