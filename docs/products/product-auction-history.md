The **Auction History** block appears on a product’s details page in Shopify admin and gives you a quick snapshot of every auction connected to that product. Use it to check whether a product is currently running in an auction, review past results, and open the related auction details without leaving the product page.

:::info

The Auction History block is available on the **Product details** page in Shopify admin.

:::

![image](/img/docs/products/product-auction-history-1.png)

## What the block shows

For the selected product, the block lists all related auctions in one place. This helps you quickly understand how the product has performed over time and whether it is currently part of an active auction.

- **Auction status** — whether the auction is currently running or has finished
- **Price** — the current price for active auctions or the final price for completed auctions
- **Start date** — when the auction began
- **End date** — when the auction ended or is scheduled to end
- **Event name** — the event associated with the auction

## Available actions

- **Preview** — opens the auction details for a specific entry
- **View all** — opens the full list of auctions connected to that product

## How to add the Auction History block

#### 1. Open Products in Shopify admin

Go to **Shopify Admin → Products**.

#### 2. Open a product

Select any product to open its **Product details** page.

#### 3. Add the app block

Scroll to the **Blocks** section and click **\+ Block**.

#### 4. Choose Reflow Auctions

Select **Reflow Auctions**. The **Auction History** block is added automatically.

#### 5. Optional: pin the block for your team

If needed, click **Pin to pages for all staff** so the block appears for other staff members as well.

:::tip

Pinning the block for all staff is useful if your team regularly reviews auction activity from the product page.

:::

## When to use it

The Auction History block is especially useful when you want to:

- Check whether a product is currently part of an active auction
- Review previous auction outcomes for a product
- Compare current and past auction pricing at a glance
- Open the full auction record directly from the product page

## Behavior notes

- The block shows both **active** and **past** auctions.
- Information updates automatically as auction activity changes.
- Only auctions related to the **current product** are shown.

:::note

If no auctions are linked to the product, the block will not show any auction entries for that item.

:::
