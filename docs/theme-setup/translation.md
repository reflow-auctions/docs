Use Shopify’s translation tools to show your storefront content in Spanish or Arabic, including text that appears in your auction widgets and related storefront components.

:::info

Storefront translations for this app are managed through Shopify’s **Translate & Adapt** app.

:::

:::warning

Before you edit or review translations, make sure the store language you want to work with has been added in Shopify and assigned to your storefront domain.

:::

### Before you begin

- You need access to your **Shopify Admin**.
- The target language must be added in **Settings → Languages**.
- To review storefront text properly, the language should be assigned to the relevant domain.

![image](/img/docs/theme-setup/translation-1.png)

### Set up storefront translations

#### 1. Add the storefront language

In Shopify Admin, go to **Settings → Languages**.

Add **Spanish** or **Arabic**, depending on the language you want to configure.

If prompted, assign the language to the appropriate storefront domain so it can be previewed correctly.

#### 2. Open Shopify’s translation tool

In Shopify Admin, go to **Apps → Translate & Adapt**.

This is where Shopify manages translated storefront content, including app-related storefront text.

#### 3. Translate the app content

Inside **Translate & Adapt**, find and select the app content for **Reflow Auctions** (or the relevant app content shown in your store).

Review the available storefront text and add or update translations as needed.

#### 4. Review the storefront

Open your storefront in the selected language and confirm the translated text appears correctly in:

- Auction widgets
- Related storefront messaging
- Other visible app content connected to the storefront experience

:::tip

If translations do not appear right away, refresh the storefront and verify that you are viewing the correct published language version.

:::

### Important notes

:::note

Shopify controls the storefront translation workflow for this app through **Translate & Adapt**.

That means you must first add the language in Shopify before you can edit and review translated storefront text.

:::

### Troubleshooting

### I do not see Spanish or Arabic in Translate & Adapt

Go back to **Settings → Languages** and make sure the language has been added to your store first.

### The translated text is not showing on the storefront

Check that the language is assigned to the correct domain and that you are previewing the storefront in that language.

### Some app text still appears in the default language

Review the translation entries again in **Translate & Adapt** and confirm the missing text has been translated and saved.
