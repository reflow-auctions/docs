Reflow Auctions must be enabled in your Shopify theme before auction widgets can appear on your storefront. Once the app embed is turned on, the app can automatically place auction content on supported pages in your theme.

:::info

Turning on the app embed automatically installs the Auctions widget on your collection pages and adds the Auction widget to product pages where auctions are active.

:::

![Shopify theme editor showing the Reflow Auctions app embed enabled](/img/docs/theme-setup/app-embed-1.png)

## How to enable Reflow Auctions in your theme

#### 1. Open your Shopify Theme Editor

In Shopify, go to your online store theme and open the **Theme Editor** for the theme you want to use with Reflow Auctions.

#### 2. Go to App Embeds

In the Theme Editor sidebar, open **App Embeds**. This is where Shopify manages app-level theme features.

#### 3. Turn on Reflow Auctions

Find **Reflow Auctions** or **Auction Embed** in the list, then switch it **on**.

#### 4. Save your changes

Save the theme after enabling the embed so the auction widgets appear on your storefront.

## What happens after you enable it

- **Collection pages:** The Auctions widget is automatically added to your collection pages.
- **Product pages:** The Auction widget is automatically added to product pages that have an active auction.
- **Live auction details:** Shoppers can see information such as the current bid, countdown timer, bid button, wishlist button, and other auction details.

:::tip

You can update the widget layout and colors at any time from the app embed settings to better match your storefront design.

:::

## Customize the widget

After the app embed is enabled, you can return to the embed settings whenever you need to adjust how the auction widget looks on your store. This includes visual options such as layout and colors.

## Remove the widget from products no longer used for auctions

If a product is no longer being used for auctions, you can remove the Auction widget from that product page. This helps keep your storefront focused only on products with active auction activity.

:::note

If the auction widget does not appear on your storefront, make sure the correct theme is being edited and that your changes were saved after enabling the app embed.

:::
